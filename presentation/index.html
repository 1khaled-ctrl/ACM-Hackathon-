GoTrain for GoPilot

An autonomous model-engineering agent, proposed as a native extension of RASID's GoPilot platform.

A — The Problem

GoPilot can analyze Earth.
But what happens when the model doesn't fit?

🌍

Regional drift

A model trained on one region's imagery, sensor mix, and lighting degrades silently in another.

🔍

No diagnosis

Customers see poor predictions, but have no way to know if it's the data, the model, or the task.

⚖️

No safe path to "fix it"

Retraining a model today means a manual ML project — not something GoPilot can offer a customer in-chat.

B — Our Solution

GoPilot tells you what Earth looks like. GoTrain teaches it what to look for next.

GoTrain is an autonomous model-engineering agent — Strands Agents running Claude Sonnet on Amazon Bedrock — that diagnoses a customer's dataset, adapts or trains a model, stress-tests it, and hands a reviewed, versioned model back to GoPilot. An engineer stays in the loop with pause, takeover, approval, and rollback at every step.

D — Why We're Different

Four advantages

01

Intelligent EO preprocessing

GeoGuard and DataIQ diagnose band stats, modality, class balance, corruption, and duplicates before a single training run starts.

02

Explainable autonomy

Every experiment is logged with a hypothesis and an outcome graded in code — not just a black-box sweep.

03

Robustness beyond accuracy

StressLab re-evaluates checkpoints under band dropout, noise, and geometric perturbation — one accuracy number isn't trust.

04

Engineer authority, always

Pause, manual takeover, approve/reject, verified rollback, and a full audit trail — autonomy with a human backstop.
